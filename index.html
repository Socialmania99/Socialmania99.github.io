Copyit

v1.0.0 · Chrome

Free Chrome extension. Save text, links, code, images and videos once, find them forever. Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no server.

  • Save, search, tag and sort clips into folders
  • Filters: All, Links, Code, Multimedia
  • Scan to open: offline QR codes for short clips
  • Import and export: JSON, CSV, TXT, Markdown
  • Multicolor or Black & White themes, Light / Dark / Auto
  • Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+Y (Cmd+Shift+Y on Mac)

Archiv AI

v1.0 · Chromium

Chat Exporter & Library. Export ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 230+ AI chats to PDF, Word, Markdown, Notion and Obsidian. Private, on-device, open source.

  • Nine formats: PDF, Word, image, Markdown, HTML, JSON, text, Notion, Obsidian
  • Private searchable library with folders and topic tags
  • Duplicate-aware saving, redaction of keys, tokens, emails, phones
  • 239-tool directory, 15 hand-written adapters, auto-detect, Teach Archiv AI
  • Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+E
